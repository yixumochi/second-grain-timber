How estimates and formal grading differ

The project rule uses approximately three supported proxy faces and 60% estimated surface coverage for a broader visual estimate. Neither case reaches that rule; it does not block seller claims, dimensional role estimates or local defect inspection.

Official structural grading considers features such as knots, slope of grain, holes, wane and warp. These saved photo estimates do not assign a certified strength grade.
