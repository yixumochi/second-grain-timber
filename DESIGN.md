# SECOND GRAIN presentation system

Refine the existing identity: paper, forest ink, reclaimed timber photographs and saved analytical figures. The primary reader is evaluating the research. Evidence provenance and qualifications should be as legible as conclusions.

## Reading order

Overview introduces the research question and saved model. Materials follows source photographs into evidence and decision scenarios. Method explains designer intent, allocation and traceability. Cases compares three independent uses of the same inventory. The archive retains detailed evidence and research history.

The main navigation is independent of optional data modules. Each chapter has one main heading, a meaningful page title and keyboard focus on chapter transitions. In-page anchors must not change the reader's chapter accidentally.

## Type and color

- Display: self-hosted Newsreader, weight 450, tracking -0.025em.
- Body and controls: self-hosted Public Sans; body 16px, controls 13–14px, captions 12–14px. Use monospace only for identifiers, code and measurements.
- Paper `#f5f3ec`; panel `#fcfbf7`; ink `#24382e`; dark evidence surface `#1e3028`.
- Secondary text `#4f6053` on light panels; `#c3d0bd` on dark panels. Use a dark rust `#8b462f` for focus and restrained interaction emphasis.
- Section headings explain content; factual figure notes follow headings. Preserve labels that distinguish observation, inference, assumptions and saved scenarios.

## Figures and interaction

Lead with saved artifacts and ordinary links. Reserve prominent controls for inspecting evidence. Respect reduced motion, avoid redrawing paused/offscreen figures, and defer response datasets until the figure becomes visible. A failed animation should expose its static result and original record.

Fixed-coordinate scientific figures scroll within their own region on narrow screens; captions and result values remain outside that scroll area. Keep the surrounding document responsive.
